Key findings

1. Database transaction matching

The review identified 37 FCMB rows and 98 Fidelity Bank rows dated in 2026. Each matched one-to-one with a 2026 database transaction after normalizing whitespace and removing the bank-reference suffix that had been appended to some database descriptions. The source and database also have no repeated exact transaction keys (bank, date, amount, and normalized narration).

This means no 2026 workbook transaction was found missing from the database under the comparison method, and no additional non-deleted 2026 database transaction was found that lacked a workbook counterpart.

2. Month coverage

Month-level row counts are detailed on page 2. Across both sheets together, there is at least one dated transaction row in every month from January through October. Coverage is not complete for each bank individually.

3. Source-field traceability

The six FCMB blank-narration rows relate to three references, each appearing on two rows: 2020251407-SELL-BONDMMDISCOUNT (5 February; ₦4,813,013.70 and ₦18,698.63), 2020280441-SELL-BONDMMDISCOUNT (5 March; ₦5,775,616.44 and ₦22,438.36), and 2020377572-SELL-BONDMMDISCOUNT (18 June; ₦5,767,389.04 and ₦23,261.10). The references aid tracing, but the underlying transaction descriptions should be confirmed from bank advice or supporting vouchers.
