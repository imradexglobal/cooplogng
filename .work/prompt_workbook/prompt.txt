A170: PHASE 3 — DEFINE THE AUTO SHARE BUSINESS RULES

A173: 3.1 Priority-based allocation
A174: The allocation order MUST come from the Priority column in Enterprise Account Settings.
A175: The intended processing sequence is:
A176: 1. Retrieve the applicable enterprise accounts.
A177: 2. Identify which accounts are eligible for Auto Share.
A178: 3. Exclude all accounts that must not participate.
A179: 4. Sort the remaining eligible accounts according to their configured Priority.
A180: 5. Process them in that order.
A181: 6. Calculate each allocation using the verified Excel allocation rules.
A182: 7. Move to the next enterprise only after completing the current enterprise's allocation logic.
A183: The priority determines the order in which eligible enterprises are processed. It must not independently change the amount due to an enterprise.
A184: 3.2 Mandatory exclusions
A185: The following enterprise categories MUST ALWAYS be excluded from Auto Share:
A186: * Compulsory Due / Compulsory Dues.
A187: * Penalty.
A188: * Revenue.
A189: This exclusion is mandatory regardless of the enterprise's Priority value.
A190: For example:
A191: ```
A192: | Enterprise       | Priority | Category             | Auto Share                          |
A193: | ---------------- | -------- | -------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
A194: | Ordinary Savings | 1        | Savings              | Eligible                            |
A195: | Compulsory Due   | 2        | Compulsory Due       | Excluded                            |
A196: | Ordinary Shares  | 3        | Shares               | Eligible                            |
A197: | Penalty          | 4        | Penalty              | Excluded                            |
A198: | Revenue          | 5        | Revenue              | Excluded                            |
A199: | Welfare          | 6        | Savings/Contribution | Eligible, subject to existing rules |
A200: ```
A201: The table is illustrative. Actual enterprise classification must be established from CofinLog's existing settings and business rules.
A202: Do not rely exclusively on enterprise names to determine these exclusions if the application already has a structured enterprise classification or account-type field.
A203: If the application does not have a reliable way to identify one of these categories, stop and report the issue. Do not guess based on a partial name match.
A204: The exclusions must be enforced in the allocation engine itself, not merely hidden in the user interface.
A205: This ensures that an excluded enterprise cannot accidentally receive an Auto Share allocation through another entry point.
