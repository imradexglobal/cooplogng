# CoopLog Tutorial Video 1
## Introduction and Login

**Recommended length:** 2–3 minutes  
**Audience:** Cooperative administrators, staff, and authorized users  
**Format:** 16:9 landscape for YouTube and in-app training  
**Tone:** Clear, friendly, professional, and reassuring  
**Video type:** Screen recording with voice-over and captions

---

## Video objective

By the end of this video, viewers will understand what CoopLog is used for, how to open the application, how to sign in, and what to do if they cannot log in.

---

## Before recording

Prepare a demonstration account with non-sensitive sample data. Do not record a real password, recovery code, private member information, or financial information. If the application displays a cooperative name, member names, account numbers, or balances, use training data or blur the sensitive information.

Recommended demonstration values:

- **Cooperative name:** `[Your Cooperative Name]`
- **Username:** `demo.admin`
- **Password:** Do not display the password on screen
- **Environment:** Training or demonstration account

If your installation uses a different login address, replace `[App Login Address]` in the script with the correct address or show the desktop application shortcut instead.

---

# Complete production script

## Scene 1 — Opening title

**Time:** 0:00–0:08  
**Visual:** CoopLog logo or application splash screen. Use a clean background and a gentle fade-in.

**On-screen title:**

> CoopLog
> Introduction and Login

**Voice-over:**

> Welcome to CoopLog, a cooperative management application for recording payments, managing members, tracking savings and loans, handling remittances, and reviewing cooperative reports.

**Caption:**

> Welcome to CoopLog: Introduction and Login

**Production note:**

Keep this opening short. Use the same title style at the beginning of future tutorial videos so the training series feels consistent.

---

## Scene 2 — What CoopLog does

**Time:** 0:08–0:28  
**Visual:** Slowly show the application landing page or dashboard. If available, use a short sequence of screenshots or a smooth screen recording of the main navigation.

Highlight these areas one at a time:

- Dashboard
- Members
- Remittance or Payments
- Loans
- Account balances
- Reports
- Settings

**On-screen text:**

> Manage your cooperative in one place
>
> Members • Payments • Savings • Loans • Reports

**Voice-over:**

> CoopLog brings important cooperative activities into one workspace. Authorized users can manage member records, record and review payments, track savings and loans, monitor account balances, reconcile transactions, and generate reports.
>
> The menus and features available to you depend on your role and permissions. Administrators may see more options than staff users, while members may have a more limited view.

**Caption:**

> Available features depend on your role and permissions.

**Production note:**

Move the cursor slowly. Avoid opening several screens too quickly. The purpose of this scene is orientation, not detailed training on each feature.

---

## Scene 3 — Open the application

**Time:** 0:28–0:45  
**Visual:** Show one of the following workflows:

### Desktop application

1. Show the CoopLog shortcut.
2. Double-click the shortcut.
3. Wait for the application to open.

### Web application

1. Open a supported browser.
2. Enter `[App Login Address]` in the address bar.
3. Press Enter.
4. Wait for the login screen to load.

**On-screen text:**

> Step 1: Open CoopLog

**Voice-over:**

> To begin, open CoopLog from the desktop shortcut or visit your organization’s CoopLog login address in a web browser.
>
> Wait for the login screen to finish loading before entering your details. If this is your first time using the application, confirm that you are connected to the correct cooperative workspace.

**Caption:**

> Open the official CoopLog application or login address provided by your cooperative.

**Production note:**

Show only the correct application address. Do not display internal development addresses, private server details, or administrative URLs.

---

## Scene 4 — Identify the login screen

**Time:** 0:45–0:58  
**Visual:** Show the full login screen. Add subtle callout boxes around the username field, password field, and login button.

**On-screen labels:**

- Username or email
- Password
- Log in
- Remember me, if available

**Voice-over:**

> On the login screen, enter the username or email address provided by your cooperative administrator. Then enter your password.
>
> Confirm that you are using the correct login page before submitting your details.

**Caption:**

> Use only the account details assigned to you.

**Production note:**

Do not type a real password in the recording. Use a prepared demonstration account and either hide the password entry or cut away while it is entered.

---

## Scene 5 — Enter login details

**Time:** 0:58–1:18  
**Visual:** Click the username field and type `demo.admin`. Click the password field and enter the password without revealing it. If a password visibility icon exists, do not click it during the demonstration.

**On-screen text:**

> Step 2: Enter your login details

**Voice-over:**

> Enter your assigned username. In this demonstration, we are using a training account.
>
> Next, enter your password. Passwords are case-sensitive, so check your capitalization, numbers, and special characters carefully.
>
> Never share your password with another person. Each user should sign in with their own account so that actions and changes can be attributed correctly.

**Caption:**

> Keep your password private.

**Production note:**

If your application uses an email address instead of a username, change the narration to “Enter your registered email address.”

---

## Scene 6 — Remember me option

**Time:** 1:18–1:28  
**Visual:** If the login screen includes a “Remember me” or similar option, highlight it. If it is not present, omit this scene.

**On-screen text:**

> Remember me: use only on a trusted private device

**Voice-over:**

> If the Remember Me option is available, use it only on a private device that you control. Do not enable it on a shared computer, public computer, or device used by multiple people.

**Caption:**

> Do not save login details on shared devices.

**Production note:**

If the application does not include this option, remove this scene rather than adding a feature that viewers will not see.

---

## Scene 7 — Submit the login form

**Time:** 1:28–1:42  
**Visual:** Move the cursor to the **Log In** button and click once. Show the loading state, then the dashboard or home screen.

**On-screen text:**

> Step 3: Select Log In

**Voice-over:**

> When your details are complete, select Log In once. Wait while CoopLog verifies your account and loads your workspace.
>
> Avoid clicking the button repeatedly while the application is loading.

**Caption:**

> Select Log In once and wait for the workspace to load.

**Production note:**

Leave the loading state visible for one or two seconds so viewers understand that a short wait is normal.

---

## Scene 8 — Confirm successful login

**Time:** 1:42–2:02  
**Visual:** Show the dashboard after login. Highlight the user name, cooperative name, and primary navigation if they are visible.

**On-screen text:**

> You are now signed in

**Voice-over:**

> After a successful login, CoopLog opens your workspace. Confirm that the cooperative name and your user identity are correct.
>
> The dashboard provides an overview of your cooperative and gives you access to the features permitted for your account.
>
> Before recording or changing any information, take a moment to confirm that you are working in the correct cooperative workspace.

**Caption:**

> Confirm the cooperative and user identity before continuing.

**Production note:**

Do not linger on real balances or private member information. Use a training workspace or blur sensitive values.

---

## Scene 9 — If login fails

**Time:** 2:02–2:32  
**Visual:** Show a prepared example of a login error, or display a text card instead of intentionally entering an incorrect password.

**On-screen title:**

> If you cannot log in

**On-screen checklist:**

1. Check the username or email address.
2. Check the password and capitalization.
3. Confirm your internet connection, if using the web application.
4. Confirm that you are using the correct login address.
5. Contact your cooperative administrator if the problem continues.

**Voice-over:**

> If CoopLog does not sign you in, first check that your username or email address is correct.
>
> Then check your password, including capitalization and special characters. If you are using the web application, confirm that your internet connection is active and that you are using the correct login address.
>
> Do not repeatedly guess passwords. If the problem continues, contact your cooperative administrator or designated support person. They can confirm your account status and help you reset your access safely.

**Caption:**

> Never share your password. Contact an authorized administrator for help.

**Production note:**

Do not show a real account lockout or expose an actual error containing private system details.

---

## Scene 10 — Sign out safely

**Time:** 2:32–2:48  
**Visual:** Open the user menu or account menu, then highlight **Log Out**, **Sign Out**, or the equivalent control. Do not actually sign out until the narration is complete, or sign out and show the login screen again.

**On-screen text:**

> Protect your account: sign out when finished

**Voice-over:**

> When you finish working in CoopLog, use the account menu to sign out. Always sign out on shared or office computers, and lock the device whenever you step away.
>
> Signing out helps protect cooperative records and prevents another person from using your account.

**Caption:**

> Sign out when you finish, especially on shared devices.

**Production note:**

If the application automatically signs out after inactivity, mention that as an additional security feature only if it is confirmed in your installation.

---

## Scene 11 — Closing and next video

**Time:** 2:48–3:00  
**Visual:** Return to the CoopLog logo or dashboard. Display the title of the next tutorial.

**On-screen text:**

> Next: Adding and Managing Members

**Voice-over:**

> You now know how to open CoopLog, sign in securely, identify your workspace, and get help if login fails.
>
> In the next video, we will look at how to add and manage cooperative members.
>
> Thanks for watching.

**Caption:**

> Next lesson: Adding and Managing Members

# Recording checklist

Before publishing, confirm that:

- The video uses a training account or demo data.
- No real password, recovery code, or private member information is visible.
- The correct login address is shown.
- The narrator explains that permissions depend on the user’s role.
- The login button and successful dashboard load are clearly visible.
- The troubleshooting steps match the actual application.
- The sign-out process shown matches the installed version of CoopLog.
- Captions have been reviewed for spelling and timing.
- The final video does not reveal internal development tools, test data, or administrative credentials.
